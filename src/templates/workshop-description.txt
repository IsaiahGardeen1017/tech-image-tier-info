[h1]Tech Image Tier Info[/h1]

Tech Image Tier Info adds useful information to Tech thumbnails, making it easier to control tech pools and beelines.

A colored border shows the technology tier.
The colors/tier mapping is from Fortnite:
    - Gray: Tier 0
    - Green: Tier 1
    - Blue: Tier 2
    - Purple: Tier 3
    - Orange: Tier 4
    - Yellow: Tier 5

Each Verticle Bar along the top indicates a tech depending on the depicted tech and the color of each bar coorsponds to the tier.

The orange dot that may appear at the bottom indicates that the tech is on the path to Mega Engineering


[h2]Ironman Compatible[/h2]
This mod just overwrites the tech image files.
